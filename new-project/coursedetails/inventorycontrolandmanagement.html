📦 Inventory Control & Management for Supply Chain

⏱️ 1 hour/day (Mon–Sat) + Sunday assessments & projects

Master inventory fundamentals, EOQ, safety stock, ABC analysis, and modern supply chain techniques – essential for operations, logistics, and SCM roles.

📅 Week 1 – Inventory Fundamentals & Deterministic Models
MondayInventory Basics & Costs
🎯 1h: What is inventory? Types: raw material, WIP, finished goods. Holding cost, ordering cost, shortage cost. Inventory turnover ratio.
TuesdayEconomic Order Quantity (EOQ)
🎯 1h: EOQ formula derivation and assumptions. Calculate optimal order quantity, total cost, reorder point (constant demand). Solve 2 problems.
WednesdayReorder Point (ROP) & Safety Stock
🎯 1h: Deterministic ROP. Lead time demand. Basic safety stock formula (service level approach). Use normal distribution table.
ThursdayABC Analysis (Pareto Principle)
🎯 1h: Classify inventory into A (high value), B, C items based on annual consumption value. Policy differences for each class.
FridayPeriodic vs Perpetual Review Systems
🎯 1h: P‑system (fixed interval) vs Q‑system (fixed quantity). Advantages and trade‑offs. When to use each.
SaturdayEOQ with Quantity Discounts
🎯 1h: All‑units quantity discounts. Calculate optimal order quantity when price breaks exist. Step‑by‑step procedure.
🌿 SUNDAY – Project 1: EOQ & ABC ImplementationExcel / Python case study
✅ Given historical demand, costs, and item list for a warehouse:
➤ Calculate EOQ and total annual inventory cost for top 5 SKUs.
➤ Perform ABC analysis on 20 items (compute annual usage value, sort, classify).
➤ Recommend reorder policy (Q‑system or P‑system) for each class.
➤ Deliver: Excel workbook or Python notebook with calculations + one‑page summary.
📝 Assessment: 15 MCQs (inventory costs, EOQ, ROP, ABC, quantity discounts).
⚡ Week 2 – Uncertainty, Service Levels & Modern Techniques
MondayService Level & Fill Rate
🎯 1h: Cycle service level (CSL) vs fill rate. Calculate safety stock for desired CSL. Newsvendor model intuition.
TuesdayDemand Forecasting for Inventory
🎯 1h: Moving average, exponential smoothing. Forecast error (MAD, MSE). Use forecast to set ROP.
WednesdayBullwhip Effect & Supply Chain Coordination
🎯 1h: Causes of bullwhip effect (demand signal processing, order batching). Countermeasures: VMI, information sharing.
ThursdayVendor Managed Inventory (VMI) & JIT
🎯 1h: VMI concept, benefits, risks. Just‑in‑Time inventory – lean philosophy, Kanban, reduced waste.
FridayMulti‑Echelon Inventory & Distribution
🎯 1h: Centralised vs decentralised. Safety stock pooling effect. Echelon vs installation stock.
SaturdayInventory KPIs & Software Tools
🎯 1h: Key metrics: inventory turns, days of inventory, obsolete %, service level. Introduction to ERP (SAP MM) and WMS.
🌿 SUNDAY – Final Project: Inventory Policy DesignStochastic demand + service level
✅ Design an inventory control policy for a retailer with uncertain demand:
➤ Given daily demand (normal distribution) and lead time of 5 days.
➤ Calculate safety stock for 95% service level.
➤ Determine reorder point and order quantity (EOQ with uncertainty).
➤ Simulate 50 days of inventory (use Excel or Python) and report stockout frequency.
➤ Deliver: simulation spreadsheet/script + a policy recommendation memo.
📝 Final Assessment: 20 questions (service level, safety stock, bullwhip, VMI, JIT, multi‑echelon) + case analysis on a real supply chain.
