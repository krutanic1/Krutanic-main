🏢 Amalgamation & Consolidation of Financial Accounts

⏱️ 1 hour/day (Mon–Sat) + Sunday assessments & projects

Master the accounting for business combinations (amalgamation) and group consolidation (subsidiaries) – essential for CA, CMA, CS, and finance professionals.

📅 Week 1 – Amalgamation of Companies
MondayNature & Types of Amalgamation
🎯 1h: Meaning, reasons for amalgamation. Types: amalgamation in nature of merger (pooling of interests) vs purchase (acquisition). Legal framework (Companies Act).
TuesdayPooling of Interest Method
🎯 1h: Conditions for merger method. Accounting: assets, liabilities recorded at book values. Calculation of purchase consideration. Journal entries in transferee books.
WednesdayPurchase Method (Amalgamation)
🎯 1h: When purchase method applies. Fair value adjustments. Goodwill or capital reserve calculation. Entry for discharge of consideration (shares, cash, etc.).
ThursdayComputation of Purchase Consideration
🎯 1h: Net assets method vs net payment method. Treatment of inter‑company holdings. Illustration: calculate consideration and pass journal entries.
FridayAmalgamation Adjustment Account & Internal Reconst.
🎯 1h: Amalgamation adjustment account (to preserve reserves). Difference between amalgamation and internal reconstruction (capital reduction).
SaturdayRevision & Practical Case
🎯 1h: Solve a full amalgamation problem (merger method): two companies combine, prepare ledger accounts and balance sheet of new company.
🌿 SUNDAY – Project 1: Amalgamation of A Ltd & B LtdPurchase vs Merger comparison
✅ Given balance sheets of two companies, do the following:
➤ Compute purchase consideration under both net assets method and net payment method.
➤ Prepare journal entries and the new company’s balance sheet using purchase method.
➤ Re‑do using pooling of interests method (if conditions met).
➤ Write a short note on which method is preferable and why.
➤ Deliver: Excel working + journal entries + final balance sheets.
📝 Assessment: 15 MCQs (types of amalgamation, goodwill, consideration methods, accounting entries).
⚡ Week 2 – Consolidation of Financial Statements (Group Accounts)
MondayBasic Consolidation Principles
🎯 1h: Control concept. Subsidiary vs associate. Consolidated balance sheet: eliminate investment against equity, calculate goodwill/NC interest (NCI).
TuesdayNon‑Controlling Interest (NCI)
🎯 1h: Proportionate share of subsidiary’s net assets. Two methods: partial goodwill vs full goodwill (IFRS). Calculate NCI at acquisition and post‑acquisition.
WednesdayInter‑Company Transactions
🎯 1h: Unrealised profit on inventory, inter‑company receivables/payables, inter‑company dividends. Elimination entries.
ThursdayConsolidated Income Statement
🎯 1h: Add parent and subsidiary P&L line by line. Remove inter‑company sales and dividends. Apportion NCI share of profit.
FridayConsolidation with Step Acquisition & Disposal
🎯 1h: Piecemeal acquisition, change in ownership without loss of control. Partial disposal – gain/loss calculation in P&L.
SaturdayRevision & Consolidation Workings
🎯 1h: Standard consolidation working table (CW) – goodwill, NCI, retained earnings. Solve a simple group with one subsidiary.
🌿 SUNDAY – Final Project: Group Consolidation (Parent + 2 Subs)Full consolidated accounts
✅ Prepare consolidated balance sheet and income statement for P Ltd with S1 Ltd (80%) and S2 Ltd (60%):
➤ Calculate goodwill and NCI for both subsidiaries.
➤ Eliminate inter‑company sales (S1 sold to P – 40% unsold).
➤ Eliminate inter‑company receivables/payables.
➤ Compute consolidated retained earnings and NCI share.
➤ Deliver: Working papers (goodwill, NCI, elimination journal entries) + final consolidated statements in Excel/Word.
📝 Final Assessment: 20 questions (consolidation workings, NCI, unrealised profit, step acquisition) + one case‑study analysis.
