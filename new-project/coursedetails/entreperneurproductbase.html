🚀 Entrepreneurship for Product‑Based Company

⏱️ 1 hour/day (Mon–Sat) + Sunday assessments & projects

From idea to launch: customer discovery, MVP, product‑market fit, business models, fundraising, and growth – essential for aspiring founders and product managers.

📅 Week 1 – Customer Discovery & MVP Design
MondayMindset & Opportunity Identification
🎯 1h: Entrepreneurial mindset. Identify pain points, market gaps. Problem vs solution. Case studies of product startups (Dropbox, Slack).
TuesdayCustomer Discovery & Interviews
🎯 1h: Lean Startup: get out of the building. How to conduct customer interviews. Build a problem hypothesis. Example interview script.
WednesdayValue Proposition & Personas
🎯 1h: Value Proposition Canvas. Create user personas. Jobs‑to‑be‑done framework. Differentiate from competitors.
ThursdayMinimum Viable Product (MVP)
🎯 1h: What is MVP? Types: concierge, Wizard of Oz, landing page, video. Prioritise features. Example: Zappos, Buffer.
FridayProduct‑Market Fit & Metrics
🎯 1h: Definition of product‑market fit (PMF). Indicators: retention, NPS, “must‑have” survey. Sean Ellis test.
SaturdayBusiness Models & Revenue Streams
🎯 1h: Subscription, freemium, transaction fee, licensing, advertising. Pricing strategies (value‑based, cost‑plus).
🌿 SUNDAY – Project 1: Product Idea ValidationProblem interview + MVP sketch
✅ Validate a product idea of your choice:
➤ Write down a problem you want to solve (target market).
➤ Create a customer interview guide and interview 3 potential users.
➤ Summarise findings – is the problem real?
➤ Sketch an MVP (wireframe or landing page mockup) to test the solution.
➤ Deliver: 1‑page validation report + MVP sketch (paper or digital).
📝 Assessment: 15 MCQs (lean startup, MVP types, value prop, PMF indicators, business models).
⚡ Week 2 – Go‑to‑Market, Fundraising, Scaling
MondayGo‑to‑Market Strategy
🎯 1h: Channels: inbound, outbound, partnerships. Launch sequence (beta, soft launch, full launch). Case: how Dropbox grew via referral.
TuesdayFinancial Basics for Startups
🎯 1h: Unit economics (CAC, LTV, gross margin). Burn rate, runway. Simple P&L for a product startup.
WednesdayFundraising (Bootstrapping to VC)
🎯 1h: Stages: pre‑seed, seed, Series A/B. Angel investors vs VCs. Pitch deck essentials. Term sheet basics (valuation, dilution).
ThursdayBuilding a Team & Culture
🎯 1h: Co‑founder equity split. Hiring first employees. Remote vs in‑office. Startup culture and values.
FridayGrowth Metrics & Analytics
🎯 1h: AARRR metrics (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue). Cohort analysis. Pirate metrics.
SaturdayPivots, Failures & Resilience
🎯 1h: When to pivot vs persevere. Types of pivots (zoom‑in, customer segment, platform). Learning from failure (case: Webvan vs Amazon).
🌿 SUNDAY – Final Project: Product Business PlanFrom idea to pitch deck
✅ Create a mini business plan for a product‑based startup:
➤ Problem & solution (from Week 1 project).
➤ Target market & personas.
➤ MVP description & product roadmap.
➤ Business model & pricing.
➤ Go‑to‑market plan (first 90 days).
➤ Financial projections (simple spreadsheet: revenue, costs, runway).
➤ Optional: create a 5‑slide pitch deck.
➤ Deliver: PDF document (2‑3 pages) + optional pitch deck.
📝 Final Assessment: 20 questions (GTM, unit economics, fundraising stages, metrics, pivots) + case analysis of a real product startup.
🧭 How to succeed in 2 weeks (1h/day + Sunday projects)

✅ Daily: 20 min concept + 40 min application (interviews, canvas, spreadsheets).
✅ Sunday projects take ~2–3 hours – they become a portfolio for incubators or job applications.
✅ After this curriculum, you can confidently discuss product startup fundamentals, validate ideas, and build a basic business plan.
📌 Recommended: “The Lean Startup” (Eric Ries), “Traction” (Weinberg), Y Combinator Startup School (free).
