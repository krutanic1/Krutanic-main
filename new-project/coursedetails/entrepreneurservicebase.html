🧑‍💼 Entrepreneurship for Service‑Based Company

⏱️ 1 hour/day (Mon–Sat) + Sunday assessments & projects

Launch and grow a service business: consulting, agency, freelancing, or professional services. Covers client acquisition, pricing, delivery, operations, and scaling – essential for founders and independent professionals.

📅 Week 1 – Service Ideation, Positioning & Sales
MondayService Business Models & Niches
🎯 1h: Types: agency, consulting, freelancing, subscription services. Identify your skills → define a niche. Case studies: design agency, digital marketing freelancer.
TuesdayValue Proposition & Brand Positioning
🎯 1h: Craft a unique value proposition. Positioning statement (for whom, what problem, why you). Create a simple brand message.
WednesdayPricing Strategies (Hourly, Project, Retainer)
🎯 1h: Pros/cons of each model. Value‑based pricing. Calculate your target rate (desired salary + overhead). Example pricing calculator.
ThursdayClient Prospecting & Lead Generation
🎯 1h: Outbound (cold email, LinkedIn, networking) vs inbound (content, SEO, referrals). Build a lead list. Outreach script template.
FridaySales Conversations & Proposals
🎯 1h: Discovery call structure. Listen for pain points. Present solution. Write a winning proposal: scope, timeline, pricing, terms.
SaturdayLegal & Contracts Basics
🎯 1h: Sole proprietorship vs LLC. Service agreement essentials (scope, payment terms, IP, liability). Non‑disclosure agreements (NDA).
🌿 SUNDAY – Project 1: Service Offering & PricingDefine your first service package
✅ Create a complete service offering for a niche of your choice:
➤ Define target client persona.
➤ Write a value proposition and positioning statement.
➤ Design two pricing options (e.g., project fee and retainer).
➤ Draft a simple 1‑page service agreement (scope, payment, timeline).
➤ Deliver: PDF document (1‑2 pages) with service package + pricing + contract template.
📝 Assessment: 15 MCQs (service models, pricing, lead gen, legal basics, proposals).
⚡ Week 2 – Delivery Systems, Finance, Growth
MondayService Delivery & Quality Control
🎯 1h: Standardise your process (SOPs). Client onboarding workflow. Project management tools (Asana, Trello). Communication cadence.
TuesdayFinancial Management for Service Business
🎯 1h: Track income/expenses. Profit margin. Cash flow management. Invoicing & payment terms (net 15/30). Simple P&L for a service firm.
WednesdayClient Retention & Referrals
🎯 1h: Onboarding experience, regular check‑ins, delivering value beyond scope. Requesting testimonials and referrals. Loyalty discounts.
ThursdayScaling: Hiring & Delegation
🎯 1h: When to hire first employee or contractor. Roles: delivery vs sales vs admin. Systems for delegation. Freelancer platforms (Upwork, Fiverr).
FridayMarketing & Personal Branding
🎯 1h: Content marketing (LinkedIn posts, blog, newsletter). Case studies & portfolio. Speaking at events. Building authority in your niche.
SaturdayAvoiding Burnout & Work‑Life Balance
🎯 1h: Setting boundaries. Time tracking. Batching work. Pricing for profit to reduce hours. Systems for automation (Calendly, invoicing software).
🌿 SUNDAY – Final Project: 90‑Day Launch PlanOperational roadmap for your service business
✅ Build a complete launch and growth plan:
➤ Niche, service package, pricing (from Week 1 project).
➤ Client acquisition strategy (channels, outreach plan).
➤ Delivery workflow (SOP for first project).
➤ Financial projections (first 3 months: expected revenue, costs, profit).
➤ One growth tactic (referral system or content plan).
➤ Deliver: 2‑3 page PDF + optional spreadsheet for financials.
📝 Final Assessment: 20 questions (delivery systems, cash flow, hiring, retention, marketing, scaling).
🧭 How to succeed in 2 weeks (1h/day + Sunday projects)

✅ Daily: 20 min concept + 40 min application (write your own documents, mock proposals).
✅ Sunday projects take ~2–3 hours – they become real assets for launching your service business.
✅ After this curriculum, you can confidently start a service company, win clients, and manage delivery.
📌 Recommended: “The Freelancer’s Bible” (Horwitz), “Built to Sell” (Warrillow), “Million Dollar Consulting” (Weiss).
